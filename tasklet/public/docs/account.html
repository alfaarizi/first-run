Account and data

Your account holds your login, email preferences, and the workspaces you belong to.

Changing your password

Open Settings, choose Account, and click Change password. You must enter your current password. Changing it signs you out of every other device.

Resetting a forgotten password

On the sign-in page, click Forgot password and enter your email. The reset link is valid for one hour, and requesting a new link invalidates the previous one.

Changing your email address

In Settings under Account, enter the new address. Tasklet sends a confirmation link to the new address, and the change applies only after you click it.

Exporting your data

Owners can export a workspace from Settings under Data. The export is a JSON file containing every project, task, label, and member, emailed to you within a few minutes.

Deleting your account

In Settings under Account, choose Delete account. If you are the only owner of a workspace, transfer ownership or delete the workspace first. Deletion is permanent after a 14-day grace period.
