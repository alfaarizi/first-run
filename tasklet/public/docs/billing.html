Plans and billing

Tasklet has two plans. Free covers small lists; Pro removes the limits and adds priority support.

What the Free plan includes

Free includes 3 projects, 5 members, and 500 open tasks per list, with labels, due dates, and assignments all included. No credit card is required.

What the Pro plan adds

Pro raises the limits to 100 projects, 50 members, and 5,000 open tasks per list, and adds priority email support. Pro costs $8 per member per month billed monthly, or $80 per member per year billed annually.

Upgrading to Pro

An owner upgrades from Settings under Billing by adding a card. The upgrade applies to the whole workspace immediately, and billing is prorated from the day you upgrade.

Downgrading and refunds

Downgrading to Free takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Projects and tasks over the Free limits become read-only rather than being deleted. Tasklet does not offer partial-period refunds.

Invoices

Owners find every invoice in Settings under Billing, as PDFs. Invoices carry the workspace name and can include a VAT number added in billing details.
