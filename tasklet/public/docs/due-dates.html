Due dates and reminders

A due date turns a task into a commitment. Tasklet shows overdue tasks in red and can remind you before the deadline.

Setting a due date

Open a task's menu and choose Set due date, then pick a day in the calendar. Due dates are day-granular; Tasklet does not schedule tasks to an hour.

How overdue tasks look

A task past its due date shows its date in red at the left of the task row. Overdue tasks also float to the top of the list until completed.

Reminders

With a due date set, choose Remind me to get an email the morning the task is due. Reminders go only to you, not to the whole workspace, and require a verified email address.

Removing a due date

Open the task's menu, choose Set due date, and click Clear in the calendar. Any reminder for that task is cancelled with it.
