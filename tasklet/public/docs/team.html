Teams and assignments

A workspace is shared by its members. Invite teammates, assign tasks, and everyone works from the same live list.

Inviting a teammate

Open Settings and choose Members, then Invite. Enter their email address; they receive a link that joins them to the workspace. Invites expire after 7 days.

Assigning a task

Open a task's menu, choose Assign, and pick a member. A task has at most one assignee, whose avatar shows on the task row. Assigning sends the member an email notification.

Roles

Workspaces have two roles. Owners manage members, billing, and workspace settings. Members create, edit, complete, and delete tasks. Every workspace needs at least one owner.

Removing a member

Owners remove members from Settings under Members. A removed member's assigned tasks stay in the list, unassigned. Removal takes effect immediately.

Member limits

The Free plan includes 5 members per workspace. The Pro plan lifts the limit to 50 members.
